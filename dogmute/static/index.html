Set up DogMute

Five steps, about five minutes. Each one gets a tick when it works.

  1. 1Make a free Discord app

    DogMute talks to the Discord app on this computer through an app of your own. You never give DogMute your password.

    1. Open the Discord Developer Portal and log in if it asks.
    2. Click New Application (top right). Name it DogMute, tick the box, click Create.
    3. In the menu on the left, click OAuth2.
    4. Scroll to Redirects, click Add Redirect and paste http://localhost . Then click Save Changes in the bar at the bottom.
  2. 2Copy its ID and secret here

    Still on the OAuth2 page:

    1. Under Client ID, click Copy and paste it below.
    2. Under Client Secret, click Reset Secret, then Yes, do it!, then Copy, and paste it below.
    Checking…

    Saved only on this computer, in ~/.config/dogmute/discord.json.

  3. 3Click Authorize in Discord

  4. 4Pick Discord's voice output

    Only what you hear from the call. Never your microphone, and nothing is recorded.

    On a Mac, DogMute hears Discord through macOS's screen and audio capture. It keeps no picture and needs no driver. The first time, macOS asks whether Python may record the screen and system audio. That's DogMute: turn it on.

  5. 5Check that it hears the call

    Join a voice call with someone, press Start the check, and ask them to say “kangaroo”. Nobody gets muted during the check.
